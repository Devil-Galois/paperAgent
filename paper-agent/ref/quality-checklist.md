# Paper Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before handing a draft to the user.

## Problem Definition

- Is the problem stated precisely?
- Is the scope bounded?
- Are key assumptions explicit?

## Evidence

- Does each major claim have support?
- Are weakly supported statements marked as tentative?
- Are references relevant rather than merely numerous?

## Structure

- Does the introduction lead to a clear problem?
- Does each section have a distinct role?
- Is the conclusion consistent with the body?

## Technical Rigor

- Are mechanisms explained rather than only named?
- Are tradeoffs discussed?
- Are limitations acknowledged?

## Writing Quality

- Are there repeated sentence patterns?
- Is the prose specific instead of generic?
- Are terminology and notation consistent?
- Are there filler phrases that can be removed?

## Risk Checks

- Any fabricated citation risk?
- Any unsupported quantitative claim?
- Any paragraph that reads like stitched summaries?
