# Source Priority and Search Guidance

## Preferred Source Types

1. conference and journal papers
2. arXiv or institutional preprints
3. official documentation and standards
4. university, lab, or author homepages
5. high-quality technical blogs

Avoid treating news articles as primary technical evidence.

## Search Strategy

For each topic, separate queries into:

- foundational concepts
- recent frontier progress
- benchmark or evaluation terms
- competing methods
- negative keywords to suppress irrelevant meanings

## Good Practices

- search both broad and narrow phrases
- track aliases and synonyms
- distinguish survey papers from original contributions
- prefer papers with strong citation context or recognized venues
- verify surprising claims with at least one additional source
